Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: TuneWalk has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your favourites, saved tours and progress stay on your phone. Your location is used to guide you on tours and is never stored or sold by us.

Who we are

TuneWalk is made by Verum Lux Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 16829396. We are the “controller” of any personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at support@tunewalk.app.

What the app uses, and why

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone’s location while you are using it to show where you are on the map, to draw walking, cycling or driving routes to stops, record stores and venues, to show distances, and (if you switch it on in Settings) to start a stop’s audio automatically when you arrive.

To draw a route, the start and end points of that route are sent to our routing provider, OpenRouteService (operated by HeiGIT gGmbH, Heidelberg, Germany), which returns the path. We do not keep a history of where you have been, and we do not use your location for advertising.

You can turn location off at any time in your phone’s Settings. The app still works, without your position, routes and automatic audio.

Audio

Tour audio keeps playing when your screen is locked, so you can keep your phone in your pocket while you walk. The app does not use your microphone.

Things saved on your phone

Your favourite stops and record stores, saved tours and events, tour progress, chapters you’ve listened to, ratings, chosen city, language and app settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Content we send to the app

Tours, stops, audio, artists, record stores, photos and events are delivered from our database, hosted by Supabase. Like any web service, our providers may briefly log technical information such as your IP address and the time of a request, to keep the service running and secure.

Maps

On iPhone, maps are provided by Apple Maps, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Events and ticket links

Event listings come from Ticketmaster and Eventbrite. When you tap “Tickets”, the event opens on their website, and their own privacy policies apply from that point.

Sharing

When you share a tour, your phone’s own share sheet is used. We don’t see who you share with.

Purchases

If you buy a city pack, the payment is handled entirely by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your card details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so we can unlock the content.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don’t sell or share your personal data.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. As the app keeps your personal choices on your own device, we will usually hold nothing that identifies you, but you can always contact us at support@tunewalk.app. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

TuneWalk is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If we change how the app uses data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
