The intro

Welcome to Punk Revolution. Between 1975 and 1979, a few hundred young people in London tore up the rules of rock music, and the noise they made still echoes around the world. Punk didn’t come from big studios or stadiums. It came from basements, pubs, squats, clothes shops and cheap rehearsal rooms, and many of them are still standing. On this tour we follow the story from the very first Sex Pistols gig to the studios where punk’s greatest albums were made, through Soho, the King’s Road, Notting Hill, Camden and Islington. You don’t have to do it all in one day. Walk at your own pace, and when you reach each stop, press play. Our first stop is the 100 Club, at 100 Oxford Street.
