FAQ & support

Need help? Email support@tunewalk.app and we’ll get back to you.

What is TuneWalk?

An audio guide to a city’s music history. Walk self-guided tours past the places where music happened, listen to the story at each stop, discover record stores, and find live music tonight.

Which cities are available?

We’re launching with London. More UK cities will follow.

Is it free?

The app is free to download. Record stores and events are free for everyone. Full city tour packs will be available as a purchase inside the app.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Your favourites and tour progress stay on your phone.

Why does the app ask for my location?

To show where you are on the map, draw your route to the next stop, and (if you switch it on) start the audio automatically when you arrive. It’s only used while you’re using the app. See our Privacy Policy.

The audio doesn’t start by itself when I arrive at a stop.

Turn on Auto-play at stops in the app’s Settings and allow location access. You can always tap play on any stop yourself.

Can I read the stories instead of listening?

Yes. Every stop has the full story as text, and you can make the text bigger with the Aa button.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, for now. Offline downloads of tours are coming.

Where do the events come from?

From Ticketmaster and Eventbrite, refreshed every day for London music venues. Tickets are sold by them, not by us.

I spotted a mistake in a story.

Thank you! Tell us at support@tunewalk.app and we’ll fix it.

Who took the photos?

Our own, and photos from Wikimedia Commons shared under Creative Commons licences. Every photographer is credited in the app under Settings › Photo credits.

I know a place that should be included.

We’d love to hear it. Email support@tunewalk.app with the stop, store or venue and what you know.

I own a record store. How do I get listed or verified?

Email us with your store’s name and address and we’ll be in touch.
